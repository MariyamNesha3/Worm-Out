The Challenges

Identifying key gameplay and engagement challenges to create a satisfying, intuitive, and rewarding worm-escape puzzle experience.

Untangling the
Escape

Worms can block one another’s paths, making it difficult for players to determine which worm should move first. The challenge is to make movement rules clear while keeping each level engaging.

A Smooth Puzzle
Experience

Responsive controls, clear feedback, useful hints, and easy retry options help players recover from mistakes and enjoy the satisfaction of solving difficult levels.

Clear Paths, Clear
Goals

Players must quickly understand where each worm needs to go, which paths are blocked, and how their moves affect other worms. Visual clarity is essential for confident decision-making.

Cute, Yet
Challenging

Players need a balance between the playful worm characters and puzzles that require genuine thought. Levels should gradually introduce obstacles and complex paths without becoming frustrating.

UI Design

A collection of the main game interfaces, showcasing the home screen, worm maze puzzles, level outros, missions, the leaderboard, daily challenges, streaks, and the shop.

Homepage
The streak is displayed at the top to encourage users to play daily, while the currency supports in-game monetization.
Rewarded ads let users earn rewards by watching ads, while the leaderboard encourages competition and motivates users to rank higher.
The bottom navigation bar includes three main sections: Home, Quest, and Settings.
The app name and tasks are designed to motivate users to play the game and complete the tasks.
The main highlights are the Level and Daily Puzzle buttons, positioned in easily accessible areas for quick navigation.

Homepage

The worm maze challenges players to plan their moves, navigate winding paths, and solve puzzles strategically. Clear indicators for lives, coins, and hints help players understand their progress and make informed decisions during gameplay.

Outro Screen

The outro adapts to the player’s performance, celebrating achievements with messages like “Perfect!”, “Level Complete!”, or “Great Job!” and encouraging them to continue to the next level.

Mission Board

The Mission Board gives players daily goals and a journey-based reward system to encourage consistent gameplay. Progress bars, coin rewards, and clear Play or Claim buttons help users track tasks, understand their progress, and collect rewards easily. The warm colors, illustrated background, and card-based layout make missions engaging and easy to navigate.

Leaderboard

The Leaderboard lets players compare their rankings with other players and track their competitive progress. Rank indicators, player avatars, and reward displays make standings easy to understand, while the dark background highlights the leaderboard and creates a distinct competitive experience. The Play button provides a clear path back to gameplay.

Daily Challenge

The feature encourages players to return each day through a puzzle calendar and monthly reward milestones. Players can track completed challenges, replay their daily puzzle, and work toward bonus rewards. The calendar layout, color-coded dates, and visible progress indicators make participation easy to understand while motivating players to build a consistent playing habit.
★★★♛

Streak

The streak feature motivates players to play consistently by tracking consecutive days of activity and showing daily progress toward rewards. The visual calendar, flame icon, and highlighted reward day make milestones easy to recognize, encouraging users to maintain their streak.

Shop

The shop allows players to purchase coin bundles, hints, and ad-free options to enhance their gameplay. Clearly grouped offers, visible pricing, discount badges, and highlighted purchase buttons help users compare options quickly and make informed choices.
